# Search for an item

## Open the home page

Click "I understand" on the cookie notice.
Verify the top menu shows "MAPS", "ITEMS" and "TRADERS" and an item price table is shown.

## Find the Salewa

In the search box on the home page, type "Salewa".
Verify "Salewa first aid kit" appears in the results, then click it.

## Item page

Verify the page heading is "Salewa first aid kit" and the page shows "Sell for", "Buy for" and "Flea price history".
Verify the Categories section lists "Medkits".
