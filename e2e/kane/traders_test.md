# Browse the traders

## Open Traders

Click "I understand" on the cookie notice, then click "TRADERS" in the top menu.
Verify the page lists the traders "Prapor", "Therapist", "Skier", "Peacekeeper", "Mechanic", "Ragman" and "Jaeger".

## Open Prapor

Click "Prapor".
Verify the Prapor trader page opens with "Prapor" as its heading and a list of items for sale.
